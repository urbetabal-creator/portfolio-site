About

Hi, I'm Urbe.
I build the systems behind the scenes.

Most businesses don't struggle because they lack tools. They struggle because the tools don't talk to each other. Leads sit in one app, tasks in another, follow-ups depend on someone remembering.

That's the gap I work in. I connect the pieces, automate the repetitive parts and organize the data so your team can spend its time on the work that actually needs a person.

How I work
Reliable, traceable, easy to hand over

The principles behind every build.

Plan before building

I document the full logic (triggers, actions, conditions and error paths) before touching a single tool.

Test with real data

Everything is built in your environment and tested with real data, so there are no surprises at launch.

Fail safe, stay traceable

Duplicate checks run first, AI decisions are logged and anything uncertain is flagged for your review instead of guessed.

Hand over properly

You get a walkthrough, written documentation and a support window so your team can own what was built.
